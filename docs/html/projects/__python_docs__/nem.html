nem module¶

NEM.py

Use of Nodal Expansion Method (NEM) to expand the flux using known:

  1. Average flux,

  2. Net currents

  3. Conservation of the 1st and 2nd moments

The solution is obtained in 1-dim.

Computes the expansion coefficients for all energy groups

This function allows to calculate the expansion coefficients for the PARCS based expanded form e.g., phi(u) = a0+a1*h1(u/a)+…+a4*h4(u/a)

The transverse leakage source term relies on the following relation:

L(u) = l0+l1*h1(u/a)+l2*h2(u/a)

where, u = x,y, or z

Parameters:

  • length (float) – the length of the node in cm

  • xs (dict) –

    includes the following keys and corresponding group values: nsfxs : group-wise fission production (nu*SigF) in 1/cm

    [1, …, G] where 1 is the fast group

    chi : fission spectrum diffxs : diffusion coefficient in cm remxs : removal cross section (rabs+out-of-group scattering) in 1/cm spmtx : group-to-group scattering production in 1/cm.

    The structure of the matrix is: 1->1, 1->2, … 1->G (from the fastest group) 2->1, 2->2, … 2->G

    keff : eigenvalue of the entire problem ng : number of energy groups

  • bc (dict) – includes the following keys and corresponding group values: avFlux : nodal average flux in n/cm^2/s wJnet : surface mean net current on the left boundary in n/cm^2/s eJnet : surface mean net current on the right boundary in n/cm^2/s nJnet : surface mean net current on the upper boundary in n/cm^2/s sJnet : surface mean net current on the bottom boundary in n/cm^2/s wFlux : surface het flux on the left boundary in n/cm^2/s eFlux : surface het flux on the right boundary in n/cm^2/s

  • trLeakage (dict) – eL : transverse leakage in y-direction (right neighbor) wL : transverse leakage in y-direction (left neighbor) nL : transverse leakage in the x-direction (upper neighbor) sL : transverse leakage in the x-direction (lower neighbor) eD : diffusion coefficient (right neighbor) wD : diffusion coefficient (left neighbor) nD : diffusion coefficient (upper neighbor) sD : diffusion coefficient (lower neighbor) edx : x-dim of the right node wdx : x-dim of the left node ndx : sdx :

  • direction (str) – solution direction - either ‘x’ or ‘y’.
